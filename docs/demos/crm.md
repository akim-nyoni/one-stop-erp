# Demo: CRM

Goal: show that leads, opportunities, activities and messages work end to end. Allow 15 to 20 minutes. Sample data loaded; signed in as admin.

## Leads and opportunities
1. Open **Capture a lead**. A prospective customer with an e-mail or phone and where they came from; an earlier lead with the same e-mail or phone is flagged. Fill in: Name, Source. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
2. Open **Work a lead**. Mark contacted or qualified, disqualify with a reason, or convert a qualified lead into an opportunity (and, with a code, its customer). Fill in: Lead id, Step. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
3. Open **Opportunity pipeline**. Move an opportunity through the pipeline (lead, qualified, proposal, negotiation) with its probability and expected close date, or close it as won or lost (with the reason); Forecast shows the weighted pipeline by stage; Timeline lists everything done on it; Convert raises the sales order from it (lines ITEM:qty[:price]; …). Fill in: Step, Stage, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Activities
4. Open **Log an activity**. A call, e-mail, meeting, task or note against a lead, opportunity or customer; give a due date for something still to do. Fill in: Type, Subject. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
5. Open **Complete an activity**. Marks an activity done with what came of it. Fill in: Activity id. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
6. Open **WhatsApp replies**. Messages customers and leads sent on WhatsApp (matched by their number), oldest first. Fill in: Message id. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Reports to end on
- CRM-PIPE Sales Pipeline: Open opportunities by stage - estimated value, win probability and the probability-weighted value. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
