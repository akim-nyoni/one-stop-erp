# Demo: Approvals

Goal: show that things waiting for your approval, delegation and approval rules work end to end. Allow 15 to 20 minutes. Sample data loaded; signed in as admin.

## My work
1. Open **My approvals**. Documents waiting for you to approve or reject.
2. Open **My notifications**. Messages and alerts for you.
3. Open **Approve or reject several at once**. Acts on every listed instance (ids separated by commas); each succeeds or fails on its own. Fill in: Instance ids (1,2,3), Action. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Approval rules
4. Open **Approval stages and rules**. A condition (e.g. Fill in: Workflow definition id, Stage sequence, Dynamic approver. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
5. Open **Set a user's manager**. The manager approves stages that ask for the requester's manager. Fill in: User name. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
6. Open **Add an approver to a document**. Adds someone to the current stage of one document's approval, alongside the stage's own approvers. Fill in: Workflow instance id, Approver (user name). Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
