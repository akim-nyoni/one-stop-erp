# Demo: Schools

Goal: show that students, classes, fees, bursaries and wallets work end to end. Allow 15 to 20 minutes. Sample data loaded; signed in as admin.

## Students
1. Open **Students, hostels and routes**. Admit a student under their guardian's account (the customer billed); set up a hostel or transport route with its capacity and fee per term; place a student in a hostel and on a route (blank takes them out) within capacity. Fill in: Step, Gender, Capacity, Fee per term, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
2. Open **Staff children and restricted students**. Staff discount: the percentage off compulsory fees for staff children while the parent is employed (the better of it and the sibling discount). Fill in: Step. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
3. Open **Top up / spend a student wallet**. A top-up is paid into the bank; a spend (tuck shop) is limited to the balance and the student's daily limit. Fill in: Admission number, Action, Date, Amount, Wallet liability account. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Fees
4. Open **Fee structures and term billing**. A fee structure per grade and term with its items (compulsory ones billed to every student in the grade, optional ones only to those taking them); Bill term invoices every enrolled student of the grade under the prefix (a rerun skips those already billed); Bill student invoices one. Fill in: Step, Fee structure code, Item amount, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
5. Open **Bursaries**. Request a bursary (percentage or value off compulsory fees) - it reduces fees only once approved by someone else with the bursary approval right, or through the approval workflow when one is defined for bursaries. Fill in: Step, Type, Expiring within (days), Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
6. Open **Sibling discount**. A family's children (sharing a guardian, in admission order) get this percentage off from the given child on, after any bursary. Fill in: From child (2 = second), Discount %. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## School year
7. Open **Academic years and terms**. Years and their terms (no overlaps). Fill in: Step, Year code, Term number, First day, Last day. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
8. Open **Term dates, optional fees and leavers**. Term dates (with pro rata, a joiner pays from the admission date and a leaver is credited the unused part of the term invoice); a student taking or dropping an optional item (lunches, clubs, trips - billed with the term, not discounted); a student's admission date; withdrawing a student. Fill in: Step, Left on (withdraw). Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
9. Open **Classes and streams**. Classes by grade and stream with capacity and the grade they move up to (blank: final year). Fill in: Step. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Reports to end on
- SC173 Student Fee Statement: Per student (and the guardian or sponsor who pays): fees billed by term, payments allocated, arrears brought forward and carried, aged; one student or the whole school. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
- SC175 Fee Billing Run Summary: Fees billed in the period by term and grade: students billed and the amount. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
- SC176 Fee Arrears by Grade: Unpaid fee invoices as at the date per student, aged, grouped by grade. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
