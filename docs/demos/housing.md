# Demo: Housing

Goal: show that the waiting list, land and stands, allocation, sales, rentals and title work end to end. Allow 15 to 20 minutes. Sample data loaded; signed in as admin.

## Waiting list
1. Open **Register an applicant**. Registers an applicant (a second registration with the same national ID is refused) and gives the waiting-list number and priority score. Fill in: National ID, Full name, Category, Date of birth (yyyy-MM-dd), Resident since (yyyy-MM-dd), Dependants, Monthly income, Current tenure, Registration date, Marital status, Marriage regime. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
2. Open **Find an applicant and their place**. With a waiting-list number (or applicant id): the applicant's status, place by seniority and score. Fill in: Category, Order, As at. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
3. Open **Applicant intake and duplicate queue**. Contact: an applicant's gender, addresses, phone and employer. Fill in: Step, Gender, Document, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Land and stands
4. Open **Land, schemes and stands**. Acquire land into land WIP at its cost; create a housing scheme; subdivide a parcel into stands ("S1:300; S2:250" — number:size m²), its cost apportioned equally or by area into stands available; sell a stand outright for cash (an invoice to the buyer, cost of sales out of stands available). Fill in: Step, Area m² (acquire), Cost (acquire) / price (sell), Delivery model, Apportion cost, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
5. Open **Stand, parcel and scheme details**. Stand: address, ward / suburb / zone, intended use, zoning, servicing phase and the services available (water, sewer, road, electricity, storm water). Fill in: Step, Parcel tenure, Parcel occupation, Scheme funding. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
6. Open **Change a stand's status**. Moves a stand along raw land → planned → surveyed → under servicing → serviced → available → reserved / offered, and after sale → fully paid → title applied → registered → handed over, never jumping a state, each move with its authority; withholds a stand (dispute or caveat) and releases it back; consolidates free stands into one or re-subdivides one ("S1-A:300; S1-B:200"), lineage kept back to the parcel. Fill in: Step, Stand number (consolidate: several, comma-separated), Move to, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Allocation
7. Open **Allocation run**. Propose a run for a scheme's available stands (seniority, score, or a seeded ballot anyone can re-run to verify); the committee rejects a pairing with a reason or substitutes the next applicant stating why the skipped one was skipped; approve it (someone else; offers go out with a deadline; with a resolution the meeting's date and members are kept), cancel it, or verify a ballot's draw. Fill in: Step, Category, Method, Date, Days to accept an offer. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
8. Open **Offers: accept, decline, print letters**. Records the applicant's answer to an allocation offer by its certificate number; a decline needs the reason and frees the stand. Fill in: Certificate number, Answer, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
9. Open **Allocation quotas**. Sets the share of allocations reserved for a beneficiary group (women, youth, disabled, elderly, civil servants), for a scheme or overall; declares the groups an applicant belongs to (comma-separated) so allocation runs can meet the quotas. Fill in: Step, Group (quota), Target %. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Sales and instalments
10. Open **Agreements**. Drafts an agreement with a party for a stand (price, terms, special conditions), records its signing with witnesses, amends it as a new version with a reason, shows its version history, or checks every stored agreement document against its hash. Fill in: Step, Type (draft), Price, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
11. Open **Instalment sales of stands**. Sign: sells a stand on instalments (deposit, rate, zero / flat / reducing-balance interest, term) and shows the schedule. Fill in: Step, Cash price, Deposit, Annual interest %, Interest, Tenure, Term (months), Yearly instalment escalation %, Signed / as at, First instalment due. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
12. Open **Stand deposits and cancellations**. Take a deposit against an allocation (held as a liability), apply it when the sale goes ahead or refund it; cancel an instalment agreement at the buyer's request (requested by you, approved by another user): what was paid less occupation rental, the admin charge and arrears is refunded and the stand repossessed. Fill in: Step, Amount (take) / NRV (cancel), Monthly occupation rental (cancel), Admin charge % (cancel), Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Ownership and title
13. Open **Transfer of ownership**. Lodge a transfer (sale, cession, inheritance, donation, court order) to a new holder; verify each requirement with its document; the checklist shows what is outstanding (rates and housing account clearances are checked live); submit, approve (by someone else), complete or reject. Fill in: Step, Transfer type, Consideration, Requirement (verify), Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
14. Open **Joint ownership**. Registers a stand's joint holders ("CUST1:50; CUST2:50", jointly with survivorship or in common), transfers part of a holder's share, records a holder's death (survivors take the share under survivorship) and shows the holders on a date. Fill in: Step, Stand number, Regime, Portion (% of the stand), Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
15. Open **Title deeds**. Apply for a stand's title, then move the application through conveyancer assigned → lodged → query raised / resolved → registered → in custody → handed over (a conveyancer is named when assigned). Fill in: Step, To stage, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Rentals
16. Open **Rental units and tenancies**. Add unit: a council rental unit with its rooms and rent. Fill in: Step, Unit type, Rooms, Monthly rent, Deposit (let), Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
17. Open **Rent tariffs and review**. Tariff: the monthly rent for a kind of unit from the first of a month, optionally only in one suburb and for one income category (the most specific applies). Fill in: Step, Unit type, Income category, Monthly rent / percentage, Effective from (first of the month). Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
18. Open **Occupants and lodger permits**. Add occupant / move out: the household living with the tenant, within the unit's occupancy limit. Fill in: Step, Tenancy number (permit: to revoke, its number), Rooms let to the lodger, Monthly lodger fee, Date / from, Permit last day. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Schemes and development
19. Open **Scheme budget and costs**. Sets a housing scheme's budget for each cost element (land, servicing, professional fees, overheads) and records actual costs against them, capitalised into the scheme's stands. Fill in: Step, Scheme code, Cost element, Amount, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
20. Open **Scheme progress**. Records survey progress on stands (pegged, diagram lodged, diagram approved — several stands comma-separated), a bulk-service servicing certificate (water, sewer, roads, electricity, storm water: % complete and stands reached), and a land parcel's valuation. Fill in: Step, Survey stage, Bulk service, % complete, Stands reached, Value, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
21. Open **Servicing cost apportionment**. Links a scheme to the servicing contract whose cost it carries and how it is shared (equally, by area, by frontage, by zoning weight); sets a stand's frontage and zoning weight; apportions the contract's cost to date to the stands; Cost per stand shows each stand's land and servicing cost. Fill in: Step, Share by, Date. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Accounts
22. Open **Receivables control per tenure type**. Instalment debtors post to a dedicated receivables control account per tenure type (instalment sale, rent-to-buy, lease with option): a buyer goes onto it when their agreement is signed. Fill in: Step, Tenure type. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
23. Open **Housing expected credit loss**. Assesses the IFRS 9 expected credit loss on housing debtors (instalment buyers and tenants) by arrears band with the default matrices, showing the allowance needed against what is booked; Post books the difference. Fill in: Step, As at. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Reports to end on
- H1 Land Bank Register: All parcels with area, cost, status and mapped area. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
- H10 Layout Plans Register & Approval Status: Layouts with the version in force, pending amendments and approval turnaround. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
- H11 Stand Creation / Subdivision Report: Stands created from each parent parcel, with size, scheme, status and carried cost. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
