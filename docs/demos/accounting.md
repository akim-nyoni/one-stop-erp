# Demo: Accounting

Goal: show that journals, the chart of accounts, tax, currencies and closing the books work end to end. Allow 15 to 20 minutes. Sample data loaded; signed in as admin.

## Journals
1. Open **Enter a journal**. Enter the lines on the grid (account, debit or credit, cost centre, tax code for the VAT return, line narrative); the totals show when it balances. Fill in: Posting date, Narrative, Journal lines. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
2. Open **Reverse a journal**. Corrects a posted manual journal by posting its exact inverse on the date given, with the reason kept on it; the original stays as it was. Fill in: Posted manual journal, Reversal date, Reason. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
3. Open **Journals awaiting approval**. Approve or reject journals others have entered.

## Look up
4. Open **Account enquiry**. An account's postings and balance, and the document behind each one.
5. Open **Analytics dashboard**. Key figures and trends at a glance.
6. Open **Group consolidation**. Combined accounts of the group's companies.

## Chart of accounts
7. Open **Add an account**. A ledger account; a control account is posted only by its own ledger. Fill in: Account code (up to 8), Name, Type, Control account. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
8. Open **Add a cost centre or department**. GL analysis codes of up to three characters. Fill in: Kind, Code (up to 3), Name. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
9. Open **Funds, projects and fund accounting**. Add or change a fund (vote) or a project (closing a project stops postings to it), or turn fund accounting on or off: then every journal line names a fund - the default fund fills sub-ledger postings - and each fund must balance within each journal. Fill in: What. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Tax
10. Open **Add a tax code**. A tax code with its first rate and the accounts it posts to; what it applies to (a goods-only code is refused on a service line and the other way round), its jurisdiction and the tax-return box it reports in (TX226 VAT return by box). Fill in: Code, Name, Type, Rate %, Rate from, Applies to. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
11. Open **VAT return**. The VAT due for a period on the company's basis - invoice date (the ledger), tax point (deliveries move invoices between periods), or cash accounting (invoice VAT due as it is paid) - with every adjustment off the ledger listed. Fill in: To, Basis. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
12. Open **Pay or settle VAT**. Clears the period's output and input VAT accounts into the VAT payable (or refundable) account and shows output, input and the net. Fill in: VAT payable account. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Currencies
13. Open **Add a currency**. An ISO currency the system may trade or hold balances in. Fill in: ISO code, Name, Decimal places. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
14. Open **Set an exchange rate**. A rate from a date; one already set for that day and type is replaced. Fill in: From currency, To currency, Rate type, Date, Rate. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
15. Open **Exchange-rate policy**. Which rate type a document type (SalesInvoice, Receipt, SalesCreditNote, PurchaseInvoice, PurchaseCreditNote, Payment) or a whole ledger (AR, AP) uses when no rate is keyed. Fill in: Document type or ledger, Rate type. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Period end
16. Open **Period close checklist**. Everything to do before a month is closed, ticked off.
17. Open **Close or reopen a period**. Sets one period's status for one posting module (GL, AR, AP, INV, POS…): Closed stops postings (a soft close can be reopened), PermanentlyClosed can never take another posting (draft or deferred journals dated in it must be posted or deleted first), Open reopens it. Fill in: Fiscal year code, Period number, Posting module, Status. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.
18. Open **Create a fiscal year**. Monthly periods from the start date (any month - e.g. Fill in: Year code, First day, Periods (monthly), Calendar. Press OK and point out the result line and the list on the right.

## Reports to end on
- GL-BS Balance Sheet: Assets, liabilities and equity as at a date; assets equal liabilities plus equity - beside the position a year earlier and the change. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
- GL-CF Statement of Cash Flows: Cash generated by operating, investing and financing activities for a period, reconciled to the change in cash. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
- GL-CFD Statement of Cash Flows - Direct Method: Cash received and paid in the period by kind - receipts from customers, payments to suppliers, sales and expenses settled in cash, taxes - then investing and financing flows by account, reconciled from the opening to the closing cash. Show the drill-through by clicking a document or account.
